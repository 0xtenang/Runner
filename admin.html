Fair-play rules

7Fair play (automatic)

Every run is decided by rules — no reviewing needed. Trust ≥ full-pay level: paid. Between the levels: partly paid. Below the void level, a fake-GPS app or a copied route: not paid + 1 strike. Too many strikes suspend the wallet automatically (and cancel its earnings still on hold). The rules are in Game settings above. Opening the log asks the dev wallet to sign once (free).
