Common questions

How do I start earning?

Sign in, get a RUNNER Genesis sneaker in the Earn tab, then tap Record and run outdoors inside your sneaker's pace zone. The whitepaper explains energy, pace zones and levels.

Why didn't my run earn?

Only live GPS runs inside your sneaker's pace zone earn, and only while you have energy. Every run is checked by the server; runs that look like a vehicle, a fake-GPS app or a copied route aren't paid. Earnings become usable 24 hours after the run.

How do I claim RUNNER to my wallet?

Earn tab → Claim. Lock it for 7 days to get 100%, or take it now and get 80% (20% is burned). You need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain for the network fee.

How do holder rewards work?

Hold at least 300,000 RUNNER for a whole UTC day and you share 2% of that day's trading in the RUNNER/IMD pool, paid in IMD. Claim it in the Rewards tab within 7 days.

I can't sign in on my iPhone home-screen app

On the home-screen app, sign in with email. Google, Apple and wallet sign-in work in Safari or Chrome at runearn.fun, and in the RUNNER app.

GPS stops when my screen locks

Browsers pause GPS when the screen locks. Keep the screen on, or use the RUNNER app (Android: runearn.fun/download), which keeps recording with the screen locked.

How do I delete my account?

You → Settings → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what's deleted and what stays on the blockchain.
